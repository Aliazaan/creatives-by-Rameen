About Me

A Designer Driven
by Storytelling

Profile Photo

"Fashion is more than clothing — it is a medium for expression, identity, and storytelling."

I am a Fashion Designer driven by a passion for storytelling, craftsmanship, and creative exploration. My work transforms concepts into meaningful fashion narratives through innovative textile techniques, material experimentation, and thoughtful design development.

Throughout my design journey I have explored handcrafted processes including flower hammering, fabric manipulation, burnout techniques, embellishment, and surface development — allowing materials to communicate stories through texture, form, and detail.

Nature, emotions, memories, and artistic expression are at the heart of my creative process. Over four years of study and making, I have developed a design language that balances artistic expression with technical precision and purposeful craft.

"A culmination of four years — where concept, craft, and creativity converge in a cohesive fashion narrative."

The thesis project weaves together every skill developed across the programme: textile experimentation, concept-driven design, pattern making, draping, and construction — unified into a deeply considered collection.

Rooted in personal narrative and artistic inspiration, each piece is both a garment and a statement. The work demonstrates not only technical mastery but a clear, evolved design voice developed over four intensive years.
