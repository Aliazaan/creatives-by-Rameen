Rameen Razi — 2025

"Fashion is a medium for expression, identity, and storytelling — every thread carries intent."

I am a Fashion Designer driven by a passion for storytelling, craftsmanship, and creative exploration. My work transforms concepts into meaningful fashion narratives through innovative textile techniques, material experimentation, and deliberate design development.

Throughout four years of study I explored handcrafted processes including flower hammering, fabric manipulation, burnout techniques, embellishment, and surface development — allowing materials to communicate stories through texture, form, and detail.

Nature, emotions, memories, and artistic expression anchor my creative process. I believe thoughtful design emerges from a balance of creativity, technical precision, and purposeful making — work that is visually compelling, conceptually rich, and rooted in craft.

Thesis
Collection

"A culmination of four years — where concept, craft, and creativity converge in a cohesive fashion narrative."

The thesis project weaves together every skill developed across the programme — textile experimentation, concept-driven design, pattern making, draping, and construction — unified into a deeply considered collection rooted in personal narrative and artistic inspiration.

Each piece is both a garment and a statement. The work demonstrates not only technical mastery but a clear, evolved design voice forged over four intensive years.
